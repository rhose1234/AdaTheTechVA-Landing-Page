Bridging Clinical Excellence with the Global Remote Economy.

AdaTheVA HealthTech Academy was founded to resolve a major challenge in modern healthcare, which is the confinement of skilled medical and allied talent to grueling shifts, physical burnout, and geographic limits.

We serve as the premier vocational launchpad for healthcare professionals and motivated non-clinical career switchers looking to transition into the international remote workspace. Through simulation-driven curriculum, hands-on Electronic Medical Record (EMR) workflows, US health system protocols, and patient navigation training, we turn students into agile, high-demand virtual healthcare operators.

We don't teach passive theory. We build globally competitive virtual assistants, care coordinators, and telehealth specialists who command international compensation and work with true professional autonomy.

Meet the Founder

Adaobi Iloduba

Founder & Lead Instructor

Adaobi Iloduba is a dually licensed Registered Nurse (RN) and Registered Psychiatric Nurse (RPN), international healthcare operations specialist, and the founder of AdaTheVA HealthTech Academy.

With over three years of direct, active experience in remote US clinical operations, medical administration, and telehealth coordination, Adaobi successfully made the leap from high-stress bedside clinical practice in Nigeria to high-earning virtual roles across international healthcare markets.

Having navigated every hurdle of the transition herself, she has dedicated her platform to demystifying the global remote healthcare market. To date, Adaobi has personally trained and mentored over 150 healthcare and non-healthcare professionals, helping them master clinical software, overhaul their professional branding, and secure roles in US, Canadian, and European digital health companies.

Instructor: 100% led and taught by Adaobi Iloduba, with no third-party tutors and direct access to the founder's active industry systems.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about training, equipment, and career support.

Do I need a nursing or medical background to enroll?

No. The program includes structured foundation modules designed to bring non-healthcare professionals up to speed on medical terminology, patient scheduling, customer care, and administrative workflows.

What equipment do I need for this training?

You need a working laptop or desktop computer and a steady internet connection. Real EMR systems and clinical software cannot be properly practiced on a smartphone or tablet.

Are classes pre-recorded or live?

The cohort features live, interactive virtual classes with Adaobi, accompanied by practical assignments, real-time feedback, and step-by-step walkthroughs. All live sessions are recorded and made available for playback.

Do you guarantee direct job placement?

While we do not act as an employer, we provide end-to-end career enablement: an ATS-ready CV, LinkedIn optimization, interview coaching, HIPAA certification, and exclusive access to our private international job group with regular leads.

Do I receive a certificate?

Yes. Participants receive an AdaTheVA HealthTech Academy Certificate of Completion upon completing the program.
